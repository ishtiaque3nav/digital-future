Privacy policy.

Version dated October 23, 2026.

Current state: The Digital Future website is in pre-launch mode. Paid checkout is disabled; it does not currently accept orders or collect payment details.

Website visits

This version is a static website hosted with GitHub Pages and has no first-party contact form, visitor account, newsletter form or advertising analytics script. The hosting provider may process IP addresses and request logs under its own terms and privacy practices. We do not set advertising cookies through this static website.

Email and customer support

If you email us, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use this to respond and resolve delivery or purchase issues. Gmail is our business email provider. Avoid sending full card numbers, bank details, passwords or other sensitive information in support requests.

Order data when checkout launches

When we enable sales, Stripe will host checkout and process payments under its own privacy terms. Stripe collects the information necessary for payments, including a buyer email address and payment-related details. Digital Future can access transaction status, purchase email, receipt/payment reference and related order information needed to verify payment, email the purchased ZIP, handle support, refunds, and maintain records. We will not receive a customer's full payment card number. The customer ZIP will be sent manually from our support inbox within 24 hours after confirmed payment.

Use, sharing and retention

We use buyer information for processing and fulfilling purchases, providing customer support, handling refunds, recordkeeping, and meeting legal obligations. We share information with providers needed to perform those functions (such as Stripe, Gmail and website hosting); we do not sell customer contact information. We retain order and support records only as needed for business, dispute, tax and legal purposes, and can review appropriate access/deletion requests subject to retention obligations.

ChipCareer OS offline workspace

The downloadable browser application stores user-entered workspace data locally in the user's browser, not in a Digital Future cloud account. Such storage is not encrypted or automatically synchronized. Export backups and avoid entering confidential employer or research information.

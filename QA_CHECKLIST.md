# Quality and publication checklist — Digital Future v1.0

## Automated structural checks
- Seven HTML pages and local assets exist.
- All local href/src targets resolve from root pages.
- Browser navigation, mobile menu and accessibility labels present.
- No checkout, Stripe secret, buyer archive, personal bank/identity data or fake customer reviews in this site.

## Manual tests (owner)
- Check desktop, tablet and mobile screens; keyboard navigation and legibility.
- Verify public `https` page opens without authentication, without error and with Digital Future name visible.
- Replace support email placeholder, verify email receipt and outbound support process.
- Review actual operating name / DBA requirements.
- Approve truthful merchant contact, shipping/digital delivery details, privacy, terms and refund policy.
- Verify Stripe activation and link to correct live merchant.
- Review product legal and technical content and clearance of names.
- Test payment-to-delivery flow before enabling public orders.

## Not yet implemented
Public hosting, domain, support contact, live checkout, live digital delivery, purchase receipts, analytics, actual customer proof, complete legally reviewed commercial policies. This is an honest pre-launch website and cannot by itself satisfy every Stripe requirement.

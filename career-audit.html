The 30-Minute Semiconductor Career Audit

A self-scoring worksheet for device, process, and characterization engineers (GAA nanosheet, FinFET, III-nitride, memory, photonics, or adjacent).

How to use it (30 minutes, one sitting)

Score each prompt 0–2  0 = no evidence, 1 = some evidence, 2 = strong, documented evidence. Total out of 20. Do it honestly — the score is for you, not a recruiter.

Part 1 — Technical evidence (5 prompts, 10 points)

  1. Can you name one device or process result you personally drove, with numbers (e.g. Vt shift, DIBL, Ion/Ioff, yield delta, defect density)?
  2. Do you have a one-page write-up of that result a hiring manager could read in 2 minutes?
  3. Can you explain the physics behind your result in three sentences to a non-specialist?
  4. Have you characterized a failure mode end-to-end (hypothesis → experiment → root cause → fix)?
  5. Is your strongest result visible somewhere public or portable (paper, patent, portfolio page, talk)?

Part 2 — Career positioning (5 prompts, 10 points)

  1. Can you state your target role in one sentence (function + technology + seniority)?
  2. Do you know the 3–5 employers hiring for that role right now, by name?
  3. Have you talked to someone doing that role in the last 90 days?
  4. Does your CV lead with measured outcomes, not responsibilities?
  5. Do you have a next-90-days plan with one verifiable milestone per month?

Scoring

16–20: Ready to apply selectively. Spend your time on introductions, not more prep.

11–15: One gap dominates. Fix the lowest-scoring part first; re-audit in 30 days.

6–10: Evidence exists but is undocumented. Write up your top two results before applying.

0–5: Build evidence first — pick one small, measurable project and finish it.

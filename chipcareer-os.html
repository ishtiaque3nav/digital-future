Questions

Know exactly what you're getting.

Does this submit applications or connect to a live job feed?

No. This is a self-directed workspace. You bring your genuine postings and decide when and where to apply.

Does it include artificial intelligence or a proprietary ATS score?

No. Its evidence translator and phrase-overlap exercise are transparent and rule-based. They do not predict interviews or hiring outcomes.

Does the app require an internet connection?

The core tool runs offline as an HTML file opened in a modern browser. You need to download the package initially.

Can I use it for interviews at a specific employer?

It provides original educational questions and general preparation. It does not contain leaked, confidential or employer-proprietary interview banks and has no employer affiliation.

How will I receive it when sales open?

After verified successful payment, the downloadable ZIP will be sent manually as an email attachment to the address entered at checkout within 24 hours. It is not an instant download. Checkout is currently disabled during pre-launch.

Delivery and refunds.

Clear terms for the ChipCareer OS downloadable product.

Pre-launch notice: Checkout is currently disabled while tax setup is completed. No orders are being accepted yet. The terms below describe how purchases will be handled once sales open.

What you receive

ChipCareer OS v1.0 is a one-time digital purchase: an offline interactive browser workspace, getting-started instructions, career playbook, worksheets and a personal-use license in a downloadable ZIP file. There is no physical shipping or recurring subscription.

Delivery method and timing

Delivery is manual by email, not instant. After Stripe confirms successful payment, we will send the customer ZIP as an email attachment to the email address entered at checkout within 24 hours. Check your inbox and spam/junk folder. The timing starts when payment is confirmed, not when checkout is initiated. Please ensure the email address is correct. If the message is missing after 24 hours, email ishtiaque3nav@gmail.com with the purchase email, date and Stripe receipt or payment reference; do not send card or bank details.

14-day refund policy

Contact ishtiaque3nav@gmail.com within 14 calendar days of purchase to request a refund, including if you purchased in error, purchased twice, experienced non-delivery, or the files do not work as described. We offer a full refund within this window upon verification of the purchase. You do not need to provide sensitive payment details. Requests outside the 14-day window will be reviewed where delivery has failed, there is a verified defect, or applicable law provides additional rights. This policy does not limit statutory consumer rights.

How refunds are issued

After confirming the order, we process approved refunds through Stripe to the original payment method. The time for the credit to appear depends on Stripe, the payment network and your bank; it may take several business days. Duplicate or mistaken charges will be reviewed and corrected. Please contact support first if you need help.

Support

Digital Future customer support: ishtiaque3nav@gmail.com. For delivery assistance, include your purchase email and receipt reference, but never share your full card number, bank account number, or password.
